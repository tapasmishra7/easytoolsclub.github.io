FAQ

Questions before you install.

Does EasySpend work offline?

Yes. EasySpend is designed around offline-first expense tracking and budgeting.

Is my financial data uploaded?

EasySpend is designed for local-first use. Your core expense data stays on your device unless optional future backup features are enabled by you.

Can I backup my data?

Backup workflows are planned for future releases and will be documented clearly when available.

Does the app require an account?

No account is required for the core EasySpend experience.

Can I track income as well as expenses?

Yes. EasySpend supports income and expense tracking for a clearer monthly money view.

Where can I download EasySpend?
