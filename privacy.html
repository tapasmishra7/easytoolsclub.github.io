Privacy Policy - EasySpend

Last Updated: June 07, 2026

EasySpend ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

1. Data Collection

EasySpend is an offline-first application. Your financial data, including budgets, expenses, categories, and notes, is stored locally on your device using a secure local database.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal financial records on external servers.

2. App Permissions

  • Storage: Used only for generating and saving PDF reports locally on your device.
  • Internet: Used for application stability, analytics, crash reporting, and displaying advertisements.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial information with third parties.

4. Data Deletion

Since all financial data is stored locally on your device, you can permanently remove all information at any time by:

  • Clearing app data from your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services

To improve application quality and user experience, EasySpend uses the following Google services:

  • Google Firebase – Used for crash reporting (Crashlytics) and anonymous usage analytics.
  • Google AdMob – Used to display advertisements within the application.

These services may collect technical identifiers such as device information, diagnostic information, and Advertising ID as permitted by Google's privacy policies.

These services do not have access to your personal financial records, budgets, expenses, or notes stored locally on your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
