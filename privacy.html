Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

EasySpend ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Data Collection

EasySpend is an **offline-first** application. All financial data, including budgets and expenses, is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data to any external servers.

2. App Permissions

  • Storage: Used solely to generate and save PDF reports locally on your device.
  • Internet: Not used for data collection.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Since your data remains on your device, we do not share any personal or financial information with third parties.
